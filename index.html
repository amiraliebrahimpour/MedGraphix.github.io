About us

Scientific visualization built from the ground up.

Clear, accurate and visually engaging communication of scientific ideas.

MedGraphix is a scientific visualization company founded by Amirali Ebrahimpour and Yeganeh Khazaei, two undergraduate Medical Laboratory Sciences students.

We specialize in graphical abstracts, journal figures, cover images, scientific illustration, and 2D & 3D design and animation.

With over 200 scientific visuals created for academic publications, all projects are custom-designed from scratch without templates or AI-generated elements.
